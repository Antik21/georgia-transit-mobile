How to get there

Bus 24 · Zugdidi to the seaside in Anaklia

Board at the railway station and get off in Anaklia. The return bus passes the railway station and terminates at Zugdidi Bus Station.

Bus 23 · Tsaishi village

From the railway station, travel towards Tsatskhvi and get off at the intermediate stop Tsaishi. The return bus from Tsaishi terminates at Zugdidi Bus Station.

Bus 1 · Railway station to the market

At the railway station, board towards Merab Baramia Street. Get off at Market, just after 102 Shota Rustaveli Street.

Fare and payment

0.80 GEL

A city bus ride costs 0.80 GEL. Routes to villages cost 1 or 1.50 GEL depending on the route. You can pay with a Bank of Georgia Visa or Mastercard, including through Apple Pay or Google Pay.
