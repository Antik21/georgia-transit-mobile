How to get there

Airport to the city centre

At the airport, take bus 10 towards the Botanical Garden. Get off at the Drama Theatre for the city centre.

Bus 10 · Airport · 1452 → Drama Theatre · 1002

City centre to the Botanical Garden

At the Drama Theatre, take bus 10 towards the garden. Get off on Bagi Street. For the return ride, board at the Botanical Garden stop.

Bus 10 · Drama Theatre · 1002 → Bagi Street · 1801

Batumi to Sarpi

Take bus 16 towards Sarpi at Batumi Bus Station or board at Tbilisi Square. Get off at Sarpi.

Bus 16 · Batumi Bus Station · 1463 → Sarpi · 1402

Fare and payment

0.70 GEL

Standard fare with a reloadable travel card, issued free of charge. You can also tap a contactless bank card or a phone with the card in Apple Pay or Google Pay. Bank of Georgia confirms this for its Visa and Mastercard cards.
