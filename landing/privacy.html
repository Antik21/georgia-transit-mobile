Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Georgia Transit handles information in its Android, iOS and web apps, in the Telegram Mini App and on this website. You can use Georgia Transit without creating a Georgia Transit account or providing us with your name, email address or phone number.

Location and route requests

With your permission, the app accesses your location while you use it to suggest a city, center the map and find nearby stops. To load stops, it sends the map area and, when you use location, your device coordinates rounded to four decimal places to the Georgia Transit server. Tile requests also reveal the area of the map being viewed. To return the right transit information, the server receives your selected city and language and the identifiers of the routes or stops you request.

Where journey planning is available, the app sends your selected start and end coordinates, rounded to four decimal places, and your trip options to the Georgia Transit server. If you choose My location, one of those points is your device location. The server uses the points for that calculation and discards them when the request finishes. Georgia Transit keeps the planner’s points and results in temporary app memory.

Product and website analytics

We use Amplitude to understand how people use Georgia Transit. The Android and iOS apps send usage events, including app opens and moves to the background, a random installation identifier, session times, and app and device information directly to Amplitude. The web app sends usage events, a random browser identifier, and session times through the Georgia Transit server. Usage events can include the way you open the web app (browser or Telegram Mini App), the map tools you use, and the city and number of routes selected. These events omit your Telegram account identifier, search text, device coordinates and journey endpoints.

If you allow website analytics, we measure page views, the language displayed and changes to it, and which app links you click. The website remembers your choice in your browser and uses a random identifier for your browsing session. You can change your choice at any time.

App analytics and diagnostics

Google Analytics for Firebase receives the city selected in the app, usage events such as app opens and sessions, app installation identifiers and app and device information. Google can estimate a general area from the network IP address. We use analytics to understand how people use Georgia Transit and which cities they select. The selected city is an app preference that you can choose independently of your device location.

Google Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports, categories of errors the app handles, timestamps, installation identifiers and technical details such as device model, operating system and app version. We use these reports to diagnose crashes and other errors. Georgia Transit does not attach device coordinates, journey endpoints or request content to analytics or diagnostic reports. Advertising ID collection and ad personalization are disabled.

Storage, hosting and retention

The app remembers preferences such as your city and selected routes and caches public transit information on your device so it can reopen faster and show available information during connection problems. This website can store your language and theme in your browser.

The main landing page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google network information such as your IP address and browser details to retrieve those fonts.

Render hosts this page and the Georgia Transit server. Connections from the production apps use HTTPS. The hosting infrastructure processes network information such as IP addresses and request details to deliver traffic and operate the service. Georgia Transit application logs include request methods and paths, response statuses and request identifiers. These application logs omit query parameters and request bodies. Hosting logs are subject to Render’s workspace settings.

Google begins deleting crash reports and associated identifiers from Crashlytics after 90 days. Google Analytics deletes individual user and event data according to the retention settings for the Georgia Transit Analytics property. These settings do not apply to aggregate reports. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request.

Transit and map data sources

  • Batumi — ThetaMaps provides the route and stop catalog and serves as a backup for live vehicle data. Live positions are used to estimate arrivals.
  • Kutaisi — Kutaisi Municipal Transport and AZRY provide route, stop, schedule and live transit information.
  • Zugdidi — the map shows approximate bus stop locations based on publicly accessible data.

The basemap uses OpenStreetMap data. Georgia Transit’s server handles map and transit data requests. To obtain map tiles, the server sends tile coordinates to OpenStreetMap. It does not send your device coordinates or journey endpoints to the public transit data providers.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can withdraw location permission in Android or iOS settings at any time. You can still choose a city and, where planning is available, use points you select on the map. To remove Georgia Transit preferences and cached data, delete the app or clear its data in Android settings. You can remove this website’s preferences in your browser settings.
