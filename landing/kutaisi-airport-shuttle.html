Kutaisi city centre to the airport

For the airport, board at the Kutaisi Tourism Center. Check the airport timetable for departures from the city before you leave.

For the city centre, board outside the arrivals hall. The scheduled journey to the Tourism Center takes about 40 minutes.

Kutaisi Travel lists a one-way fare of GEL 5, payable by bank card. Fare and payment · Kutaisi Travel
